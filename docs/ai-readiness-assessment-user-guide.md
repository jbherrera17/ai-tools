# AI Readiness Assessment — User Guide

Status: describes a page that is planned but not yet live at `ai.jbherrera.com/ai-readiness`. This guide will move (or be linked) into the site's own help content once the page ships.

## What this is

A short, honest assessment of where your business actually stands on AI readiness — not a sales pitch dressed up as a quiz. You answer 18 questions about your business, your team, your systems, and your data. In return you get a scored report across six areas, a plain-language explanation of what's holding you back and what to do first, a plan for getting your team up to speed, and a few concrete AI projects worth considering — each with a realistic price range, not a guess.

It takes about 8 minutes. Your score is free. The full written report costs nothing but your email address.

## Two ways to start

### Option 1 — Take the assessment

Click **Start the assessment**. Higgins (your guide through this) will ask you 18 short questions, one at a time, across six areas:

- **Business Alignment** — how clear your values, vision, and positioning are
- **Infrastructure & Tech Stack** — whether your current systems can connect to anything new
- **Culture & Leadership** — whether your team will actually adopt something new
- **Data Quality & Access** — whether your information is in good enough shape to be useful
- **Use Case Clarity** — whether you have a specific problem worth pointing AI at
- **Resourcing & Budget** — whether you're positioned to fund and carry an AI initiative

For every question, either click one of the four suggested answers or type your own in plain words. "I don't know" is a completely fine answer — it tells the assessment something real, and pretending otherwise would make your report less accurate, not more encouraging.

When you finish, you'll see your **score out of 100**, your **stage on the AI Readiness Spectrum**, and a short verdict for each of the six areas — all free, no email required.

To see the full written report — specific findings, a step-by-step action plan in the order that actually matters, a team upskilling plan, and a set of suggested AI projects with pricing — enter your email and company name. You'll also be able to download the whole thing as a PDF.

### Option 2 — Upload a previous report

Already took this assessment — here or on Synergi AI's site — and saved the PDF? Click **Upload a previous report** and choose that file instead of starting over.

This restores your exact report: same score, same findings, same suggested projects and pricing, exactly as they were the day you generated it. No email required — you already unlocked this report once.

**What file works:** the PDF you downloaded from this assessment (via the "Download PDF" button), unedited. A PDF you printed from a screenshot, re-saved through another program, or edited won't work — the restore relies on a small machine-readable copy of your results embedded in the original file, and anything that re-processes the PDF is likely to strip that out.

**What doesn't work:** a report generated before this upload feature existed won't have that embedded copy. If upload fails, you'll get a clear message rather than a broken page — and you're welcome to just retake the assessment; it's short.

## What you'll see either way

- A **radar chart** across the six areas, so you can see your shape at a glance, not just a single number.
- Your **overall score and stage** on the spectrum, from "Overwhelmed" through "Orchestrated."
- **What we saw** and **what to do** for each of the six areas, grounded in what you actually said — not generic advice.
- **What to do, in order** — a short sequence of next steps, because the order matters more than the list. Fixing your data before you name a use case, for example, is usually backwards.
- **Upskilling your team** — who on your team needs what, based on how you described your culture and who'd actually own an AI initiative.
- **Suggested AI projects** — 2 to 4 specific projects tied to the bottleneck you described, each with a recommended plan and a real price range, not an invented figure.

## Privacy

- The assessment itself doesn't ask for your email until you choose to unlock the full report — your score is visible before you give us anything.
- Uploading a PDF doesn't store it anywhere. It's read once, in the moment, to rebuild your report on screen, and that's the end of its life on our servers.
- If you unlock a full report (by entering your email), that gets recorded so JB can follow up if it seems useful — same as it would if you filled out a contact form. We don't sell or share your details.

## Frequently asked

**Can I upload someone else's report?**
Nothing stops you technically, but the report and any follow-up would reflect their business, not yours. If you want your own read on things, take the assessment yourself — it's 8 minutes.

**Does uploading an old report create a new inquiry with JB?**
No. Uploading just restores what you already have on screen. If you want to talk to JB about it, use the "Talk through your results with JB" link on the report itself, same as you would with a freshly generated one.

**Will the pricing on an old report still be accurate?**
The report shows pricing as it was the day you generated it. If it's been a while, worth double-checking current pricing before assuming it still holds — plans and packages can change.

**I don't have my PDF anymore. Can I get my old report back another way?**
Not currently — nothing is stored on our end that lets us regenerate a lost report from just your email or company name. The PDF is the only copy. If you've lost it, the fastest path back is retaking the assessment.

## Questions

If anything here doesn't match what you're actually seeing, or something breaks, reach out to JB directly — the "Talk through your results with JB" link on the report is the fastest way once you have a report to reference.
